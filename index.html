About the Company

Delhi's Trusted Pouch Maker Since 2017

Founded by a team of packaging specialists in New Delhi, Techno Fab Engineers has grown from a local manufacturer into a trusted supply partner for food brands, FMCG companies, and spice traders across India.

With a GST-registered manufacturing setup, turnover in the ₹5–25 Cr range, and a catalogue spanning 15+ pouch types, we bring industrial quality and brand-ready printing under one roof.

  • Food-grade certified materials — safe for direct food contact and long shelf life
  • Custom branding & printing — multicolour rotogravure printing on every order
  • In-house manufacturing — full control from lamination to slitting to pouch making
  • Bulk & small-run flexibility — minimum order quantities suited for both SMEs and large brands
